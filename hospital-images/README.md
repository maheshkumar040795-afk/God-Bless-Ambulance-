- Format: `.jpg`, landscape, about 800×500 px, under 150 KB (compress at squoosh.app or tinyjpg.com)
- Use only photos you own or have permission to use (your team's own photos, or photos the hospital gave you).
- No photo = the card shows the red hospital icon.
